The Riemann hypothesis [was announced as (likely) proven today](https://alpo.ge/remark.pdf). I've always been an amateur at math, but I understand how groundbreaking this result is.

It's not entirely surprising that we've been getting more of these huge advancements in math and physics ever since [Navier-Stokes was solved](https://openai.com/index/navier-stokes-solution/). Yet, it feels so surreal since the Riemann hypothesis was basically THE most famous unsolved math problem for over 150 years. It has eluded the brightest minds in math, and I think it's now safe to say that frontier models have surpassed human intelligence in some "spiky" areas such as this.

With 2 Millennium Prize Problems solved in a little over 2 months, it seems absolutely unfathomable what discoveries await us in the very near future. Today, like many other days in 2026, I feel excited and terrified of the ["interesting times"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/May_you_live_in_interesting_times) that await us.
